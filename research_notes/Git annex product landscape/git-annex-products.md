# Git-annex product landscape (as of 2026-10-24)

Scope: third-party products and apps that use git-annex as the storage or dedup layer, or that sell setup/support/hosting around it. git-annex’s own assistant/webapp is not covered in depth. Image Annex (`github.com/InTEGr8or/image-annex`) is the user’s project and is excluded from the competitor list. An unauthenticated GitHub API lookup for that repo on 2026-10-24 returned HTTP 404, so it is not a public listing competitors would find — [GitHub API](https://api.github.com/repos/InTEGr8or/image-annex).

Classification used below: **(a)** a company or paid service whose product is git-annex-based; **(b)** open-source or freely hosted software that wraps or stores data with git-annex and is not sold as a product.

## Which companies or consultants sell a git-annex product, appliance, or managed archive?

### Takeaway
No company, consultant practice, appliance, or managed-archive service whose product is git-annex was found as of 2026-10-24. The things that look like products are either free software (git-annex itself, DataLad), free academic hosts (GIN, OpenNeuro, DANDI), or unrelated businesses whose names contain “annex.”

### Cited Findings
- git-annex is free software, written in Haskell, with no pricing, SKU, or support-contract page on its site. The site lists version 10.20261020 under recent news (page crawled 2026-10-20) and documents a 2026-07-17 release, so the upstream project is actively maintained, but it is not a vendor — [git-annex](https://git-annex.branchable.com/); [version 10.20260717](https://git-annex.branchable.com/news/version_10.20260717/).
- The project’s own “related software” page (crawled 2026-07-02) lists only free software, academic platforms, and cultural projects. It does not list a company, appliance, NAS image, or paid support offering — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- The testimonials page is old user quotes (2012–2013) plus Joey Hess’s personal dial-up/cabin use. It does not name a customer of a git-annex product company — [testimonials](https://git-annex.branchable.com/testimonials/).
- DataLad, the main layer people build on git-annex, describes itself as “free and open source,” tells users to get help on Matrix, office hours, or GitHub issues, and says development was funded by NSF, BMBF, NIH/ReproNim, EU Horizon 2020, and DFG grants. No paid plan or support contract is listed — [datalad.org](https://datalad.org/).
- GIN (G-Node Infrastructure) is a free web repository for neuroscience data, built on Git, git-annex, and Gogs, run as a G-Node service. It says you can set up your own server. The contact line is “if you have specific data management or data sharing requirements that cannot be dealt with using our standard services, please contact us at info@g-node.org,” which is an offer of academic help, not a published price list — [GIN home](https://gin.g-node.org/); [GIN about](https://gin.g-node.org/G-Node/Info/wiki/about); [G-Node tools](https://www.g-node.org/tools-and-services).
- Built-in special remotes include S3, Amazon Glacier, WebDAV, rclone, rsync, borg, bup, directory, and others. Those are protocols inside git-annex for talking to storage the user already has. They are not a git-annex hosting product — [special remotes](https://git-annex.branchable.com/special_remotes/).
- Name collisions that are not git-annex: Annex.com is an O’Neil Software marketplace for record centers and shredding, built with Next.js, Directus, GraphQL, and Stripe — [Directus case study](https://directus.io/built-with-directus/annex-organizational-bliss); [i-SIGMA member news, 2024-12-13](https://isigmaonline.org/blog/member-news-annex-com-enables-record-centers-to-win-customers-24-7-with-global-e-commerce-access/). Annexus Technology, Anavon, and Blunix are generic IT/Linux managed-hosting firms with no git-annex mention in the pages returned for “git-annex” plus consulting/managed/support — [Annexus](https://www.annexustech.ca/support-services); [Blunix](https://www.blunix.com/linux-managed-hosting.html).
- An aggregator page titled “git-annex in 2026: Pricing, Plans and Alternatives” (EZToolset, 2026-10-11) lists a “free plan” and a 7.7 rating. It points at git-annex.branchable.com and describes the free-software project. It is not a vendor price sheet — [EZToolset](https://eztoolset.com/tools/git-annex/).
- Searches that returned no seller of a git-annex product, appliance, or managed archive: `git-annex commercial product OR service OR company` (2026-10-24; hits were the project site, EZToolset, AlternativeTo, and the unrelated Annex.com); `git-annex consulting OR managed OR support contract OR appliance OR startup`; `"git-annex" (consultant OR consulting OR "we offer" OR SaaS OR "hosted git-annex")`. The last query returned ordinary Git/GitLab consultancies (MeteorOps, IDEA GitLab Solutions, MentorCruise) that do not mention git-annex — [MeteorOps](https://meteorops.com/technologies/git); [IDEA GitLab](https://gitlab.consulting/en-gb/); [MentorCruise](https://mentorcruise.com/consultant/git/).

### Inferences
- There is no evidenced commercial category “git-annex appliance,” “git-annex NAS,” or “managed git-annex archive” comparable to a paid Immich host or a Synology package. People who want git-annex buy ordinary disks or S3/Glacier/Backblaze and run the free tool.
- GIN’s “contact us” line could hide informal paid help, but nothing on the public pages prices that, so it does not count as a product.
- Joey Hess’s assistant work (the 2012–13 assistant is part of git-annex, not a third-party product) did not turn into a company offering found in these searches. The assistant is upstream software — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).

### Gaps
- No crawl of the git-annex forum archives for one-off “I will install this for you” posts. A lone forum offer would be a forum post, not a product, but it was not exhaustively searched.
- Non-English consultancies and private invoices would not show up in these queries.
- Whether any GIN or DataLad core developer freelances was not established. The public support channels are community and grant-funded.

## Which open-source apps use git-annex as their file store?

### Takeaway
The maintained ecosystem is scientific data tooling, not consumer apps. DataLad is the actively developed front end. GIN, OpenNeuro, and the DANDI DataLad mirror are live hosts or access layers on git-annex. Forgejo-aneksajo is a maintained 2026 soft-fork of the Forgejo git forge. Desktop and cultural projects on the official list are mostly small, and several have not been committed to in years.

### Cited Findings
- **DataLad (b, maintained).** “Building on top of Git and git-annex,” it version-controls large files as datasets, with clone/get/drop/push, subdatasets, and a CLI plus a GUI mentioned on the homepage. GitHub `datalad/datalad` was pushed 2026-10-21 (not archived, 669 stars at query time). The installer repo was pushed 2026-09-24 — [datalad.org](https://datalad.org/); [GitHub datalad/datalad](https://github.com/datalad/datalad); [datalad-installer](https://github.com/datalad/datalad-installer). Handbook pages still document git-annex adjusted mode, encryption, and annex.largefiles as the content store — [adjusted mode](https://handbook.datalad.org/en/latest/basics/topic/adjustedmode-nosymlinks.html); [encrypted annex](https://handbook.datalad.org/en/latest/usecases/encrypted_annex.html).
- **GIN (b, hosted service, free).** Live site: web repository store using Git, git-annex, and Gogs, with a web UI, a local GIN client, WinGIN, and DataLad. Self-host is documented. Not a paid product. A 2020 G-Node project page describes the same architecture; the service homepage itself was reachable on 2026-10-24. No separate “last server release” date was retrieved — [GIN](https://gin.g-node.org/); [GIN wiki](https://gin.g-node.org/G-Node/info/wiki); [G-Node projects](https://g-node.github.io/).
- **OpenNeuro (b, hosted scientific repository, free).** Docs updated 2026-10-21: “The underlying format for dataset is a git-annex repo created with datalad.” Public file content is a preconfigured S3 special remote. Private or draft data uses a project script, `git-annex-remote-openneuro`. This is a research-data host, not a photo product and not a priced SaaS — [OpenNeuro git access](https://docs.openneuro.org/git).
- **DANDI Archive (b, free; git-annex is the DataLad access layer, not the only blob store).** Docs updated 2026-09-17: dandisets are mirrored as DataLad datasets under github.com/dandisets, and “DataLad already encodes this Dandiset-version-to-S3-location mapping inside git-annex.” The GitHub org readme (crawled 2026-10-16) describes the superdataset as access to on the order of a petabyte of neural data, with blobs in the DANDI S3 archive. DANDI’s own CLI/API is a separate download path — [DANDI downloading](https://docs.dandiarchive.org/user-guide-using/accessing-data/downloading/); [dandi/dandisets](https://github.com/dandi); [dandisets org](https://github.com/dandisets).
- **Forgejo-aneksajo (b, maintained into 2026).** Soft fork of Forgejo that adds git-annex integration, derived from neuropoly/gitea. Wiki edited 2025-05-09. Releases are tagged `v<forgejo>-git-annex<n>`. CI on Codeberg shows `v14.0.3-git-annex2` activity on 2026-03-16. A demo is advertised at demo.forgejo-aneksajo.org. It is a git forge, not a media gallery, and it is not sold — [wiki](https://codeberg.org/forgejo-aneksajo/forgejo-aneksajo/wiki); [org](https://codeberg.org/forgejo-aneksajo); [actions](https://codeberg.org/forgejo-aneksajo/forgejo-aneksajo/actions). Also listed on the upstream related-software page — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- **git-annex-browser (b, maintained).** Terminal UI for browsing git-annex repositories. `janttsu/git-annex-browser` pushed 2026-10-04 — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/); [GitHub](https://github.com/janttsu/git-annex-browser).
- **AnnexRemote (b, maintained library, not an app).** Python helper for external special remotes. `Lykos153/AnnexRemote` pushed 2026-10-11 — [GitHub](https://github.com/Lykos153/AnnexRemote); [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- **magit-annex (b, maintained).** Emacs Magit extension. `magit/magit-annex` pushed 2026-03-15 — [GitHub](https://github.com/magit/magit-annex); [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- **rf (b, small, updated 2026).** “Minimalist framework for reproducible computational workflows” using git and git-annex. `apuapaquola/rf` pushed 2026-07-29 — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/); [GitHub](https://github.com/apuapaquola/rf).
- **git-annex-turtle (b, macOS Finder integration, last code push 2025-06-10).** Badges, contextual menus, menu-bar icon. MIT. Not archived, but no 2026 commit was returned by the API — [GitHub](https://github.com/andrewringler/git-annex-turtle); [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- **git-annex-metadata-gui (b, dormant).** Qt GUI for annex metadata, including an image/text preview. Last push 2017-08-23. Still packaged only as a very old NeuroDebian build (0.0.0~pre1) aimed at Debian stretch / Ubuntu 16.04 on a page crawled 2026-10-02, which is evidence the distro package was not kept current — [GitHub](https://github.com/alpernebbi/git-annex-metadata-gui); [NeuroDebian](https://neuro.debian.net/pkgs/git-annex-metadata-gui.html).
- **git-annex-adapter (b, library, stale).** Python bindings used by the metadata GUI. Last push 2022-11-16 — [GitHub](https://github.com/alpernebbi/git-annex-adapter).
- **darktable-git-annex (b, dormant integration, not a photo app).** “git-annex integration for darktable.” Last push 2021-08-19. Upstream still links it. It does not replace darktable’s storage; it is a bridge — [GitHub](https://github.com/xxv/darktable-git-annex); [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- **git-annex-watcher (b, abandoned).** Desktop status icon. Last push 2012-11-23 — [GitHub](https://github.com/rubiojr/git-annex-watcher).
- **Emacs Org mode (b, feature inside Org, not a product).** The related-software page says Org can auto-commit attached files to git-annex. No separate app — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- **Baobáxia (b, cultural network, code dormant).** Brazilian Mocambos project using git-annex to sync community repositories over satellite or sneakernet. `RedeMocambos/baobaxia` last push 2020-10-08 (description: app to publish content on the Baobáxia network). Still listed by upstream — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/); [GitHub](https://github.com/RedeMocambos/baobaxia).
- **Isuma Media Players (b, cultural project, currency not re-verified).** Upstream quote: uses git-annex to “create a two-way, distributed content distribution network for communities with poor connexions to the internet.” Docs host linked from the related-software page. No 2026 commit or company status was fetched — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- **Densho (b, nonprofit internal use, not a product for sale).** Upstream says Densho uses git-annex for “upwards of 10+ terabytes” moved from cultural-heritage sites on USB drives, with a Django web UI at github.com/densho. That is an institution’s own archive tool, not a commercial offering. The Django app’s current code was not re-checked in this pass — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- **Libraries and one-off utilities named on the same page, not products:** `gadu` is a forum post about a du-like tool, not a shipped app — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/) links the forum thread. `sizes` is a Haskell du with a `-A` switch. `Git::Annex` is a Perl interface. `easy-git-annex` is a TypeScript/npm interface. `borg2annex` converts borgbackup archives into a git-annex repo. `recastex` is a wiki tip for publishing a podcast from an annex to a phone, not a mobile git-annex client and not a company — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/). Maintenance dates for sizes, Git::Annex, easy-git-annex, borg2annex, and recastex were not retrieved.
- **No GNOME or KDE file-manager product** appears on the related-software page. A search for `git-annex-turtle OR git-annex-metadata-gui OR "gnome" git-annex OR dolphin annex` returned the Qt metadata GUI, the Mac Finder app, and the old NeuroDebian package. It did not return a Nautilus, Dolphin, or Plasma project — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).

### Inferences
- If “who else ships something on git-annex” means a maintained application, the answer is DataLad plus the scientific hosts that speak DataLad/git-annex (GIN, OpenNeuro, DANDI’s mirror), plus a Forgejo fork and a handful of UI/library wrappers. Consumer backup suites and NAS vendor apps are absent from the primary list.
- Several names on the official related-software page are historical. Listing there is not evidence of 2026 maintenance. The GitHub push dates above are the maintenance evidence.
- OpenNeuro and DANDI are the closest things to “hosted services built on git-annex,” and both are free scientific repositories. DANDI’s bytes live in S3; git-annex tracks which blob is which file. That is a real git-annex deployment, not a git-annex company.

### Gaps
- DataLad’s “in the wild” catalog (`datalad.org/in-the-wild.html`) was not opened, so smaller DataLad extensions (OSF, figshare, and similar) are not itemized here. They are extensions of DataLad, not separate paid products, per the homepage’s description of export to GitHub, Figshare, Dropbox, and S3 — [datalad.org](https://datalad.org/).
- Densho’s Django app and the Isuma docs were not re-read, so it is possible either project has stopped using git-annex since the related-software blurb was written.
- `gadu` was not opened; the related-software page itself frames it as a forum utility.

## Is there any photo, video, or personal-media product whose storage layer is git-annex?

### Takeaway
No commercial or open-source photo, video, or personal-media product was found whose storage layer is git-annex. What exists are personal write-ups, one dormant darktable bridge, a cultural-video distribution project (Isuma), and a 2024 Reddit user proposing git-annex as a backup beside Immich. Image Annex is excluded as the user’s own project.

### Cited Findings
- The official related-software list has no gallery, DAM, Immich-like, Lightroom-like, or NAS photo app. The only photo-adjacent entries are the darktable integration and the metadata GUI’s image preview — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- `xxv/darktable-git-annex` is an integration, last pushed 2021-08-19, not a product that stores a library by itself. A 2022 LWN comment describes a personal workflow (annex the raws, commit the XMP sidecar) and is a comment, not a product — [GitHub](https://github.com/xxv/darktable-git-annex); [LWN comment](https://lwn.net/Articles/882225/).
- Personal blogs, not products: “git-annex for Photography” (aaron.na31.org, page dated 2026-08-14) is one person’s CLI workflow and explicitly is not the git-annex assistant — [aaron.na31.org](https://aaron.na31.org/git-annex_photography). “Managing Gigabytes of Images with git-annex” (switowski.com, 2024-03-13) is a family-blog author moving images off GitLab — [switowski.com](https://switowski.com/blog/git-annex/).
- Forum post, not a product: “Backing up photos to the cloud” on the git-annex forum is a user with ~15,000 photos asking how to use an S3 remote. The wiki page stamp is 2025-01-06 (wiki systems often restamp; the post is a user thread either way) — [forum](https://git-annex.branchable.com/forum/Backing_up_photos_to_the_cloud).
- Reddit, not a product: r/immich post “Using Immich with Git Annex for backup” (2024-10-04, score 3) is a user planning to point git-annex at an Immich library and copy it to S3/Glacier. - Searches run 2026-10-24 that returned no commercial photo-archive product on git-annex: `"git-annex" photo gallery OR immich OR NAS appliance` (NAS hits were Immich-on-UGREEN/Synology/QNAP buyers’ guides with no git-annex); `"built on git-annex" OR "uses git-annex" OR "powered by git-annex" photo OR gallery OR backup` (blogs, the LWN comment, the forum thread). ### Inferences
- A person can archive photos with stock git-annex (S3, Glacier, removable drives). Nobody in the sources is selling that as a photo product, and no maintained open-source gallery uses git-annex as its library format the way Immich uses its own upload directory plus Postgres.
- The darktable bridge being untouched since 2021 is consistent with “workflow tip,” not “photo platform.”

### Gaps
- GitHub code search (as opposed to repo name/description) was not completed because of the 403. A private or oddly named gallery could exist. Nothing in the git-annex related-software page, which is the maintainer’s own list as of the 2026-07-02 crawl, names one.
- PhotoPrism, Immich, LibrePhotos, and Pigallery2 documentation was not re-read for a hidden git-annex backend. The searches that pair those product words with git-annex only found users backing Immich up with git-annex, which implies Immich’s store is not git-annex.

## Any Android or mobile client whose backend is git-annex?

### Takeaway
There is no current third-party Android or iOS client that uses git-annex as its backend. The supported Android path is upstream git-annex inside Termux or Nix-on-Droid. The old dedicated Android APK is officially deprecated and frozen on Android 4.x/5.0.

### Cited Findings
- Current install instructions: git-annex runs on Android inside Termux or Nix-on-Droid, including the assistant, without root. Alternatively the computer runs git-annex and uses adb to sync the device. The webapp is started with `git-annex webapp` inside Termux. Nix-on-Droid is the command-line option; its webapp needs `--listen 127.0.0.1` — [Android](https://git-annex.branchable.com/Android/); [install/Android](https://git-annex.branchable.com/ikiwiki.cgi?do=goto&page=install%2FAndroid).
- The dedicated Android app is deprecated: “This app is being deprecated in favor of Termux. It doesn’t work with newer versions of Android. These builds are not being updated.” Published APKs are only for Android 5.0, 4.4/4.3, and 4.0–4.2, hosted at downloads.kitenet.net. The install page says the old app “is no longer being updated” — [oldapp](https://git-annex.branchable.com/install/Android/oldapp/); [Debian copy of the same page in git-annex 10.20250416-2](https://sources.debian.org/src/git-annex/10.20250416-2/doc/install/Android/oldapp.mdwn).
- That old app was upstream (Joey Hess’s git-annex), not a third-party client. A design-blog note from the assistant period describes opening the webapp, a default `/sdcard/annex` repo, and a camera repository in the “source” preferred-content group. It is historical and lives in the git-annex source tree — [day 254 Android app polishing, packaged in git-annex 10.20251029-1](https://sources.debian.org/src/git-annex/10.20251029-1/doc/design/assistant/blog/day_254__Android_app_polishing.mdwn).
- `adb` is a built-in special remote so a desktop can pull and push an Android device. It is not a phone app — [special remotes](https://git-annex.branchable.com/special_remotes); [Android](https://git-annex.branchable.com/Android/).
- A forum post “[android] [adb] Support for adb over tcp” was listed on the homepage among recent posts (about seven months before the 2026-10-20 crawl). That is a user/support thread, not a client — [git-annex home](https://git-annex.branchable.com/).
- recastex, on the related-software page, “publishes podcasts and other files from git-annex to your phone.” The phone is a destination, not a git-annex backend — [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- No iOS app is listed on the Android page, the old-app page, or the related-software page — [Android](https://git-annex.branchable.com/Android/); [related software](https://git-annex.branchable.com/related_software/).
- The 2026-10-24 web search `git-annex android app client 2024 OR 2025 OR 2026` returned the Termux/Nix-on-Droid instructions and the deprecated APK. It did not return a Play Store client or a third-party mobile backend.

### Inferences
- “Android support” in 2026 means a Linux terminal environment on the phone running the same git-annex binary, or a desktop driving the phone over adb. There is no maintained graphical mobile product, paid or free, whose storage engine is git-annex.
- The 2013-era camera-repository feature in the old APK shows upstream once targeted phone photos directly. That app is frozen and does not run on current Android, so it is not a competing photo product today — [oldapp](https://git-annex.branchable.com/install/Android/oldapp/).

### Gaps
- A separate query for “git-annex iOS” or “git-annex iPad” was not run. Absence of an iOS client is inferred from the official platform and related-software pages, not from an App Store search.
- F-Droid was not searched by name. The Termux install path is what upstream documents, and the old APKs are sideloaded from kitenet.net, not described as a store listing — [oldapp](https://git-annex.branchable.com/install/Android/oldapp/).
- The GitHub search for Android repos in name/description hit HTTP 403, so a small unpublished client could have been missed. Nothing on the maintainer’s related-software list names one.
